PASEOS branch facts from Tawaraya Co., Ltd., the operator’s store locator and digital flyer directory, and cited address authorities.
Branch identifiers, names, addresses, numeric trading times and lifecycle facts are normalized independently. Publisher HTML, images, promotional copy and embedded map data are excluded.
Published points by Overture Maps Foundation (Meta, CDLA-Permissive-2.0; Copyright 2024 Foursquare Labs, Inc., Apache 2.0).
Geometry is filtered for exact branch/site identity and represented as WGS84 latitude/longitude. Overture dataset and provider record references are retained. Site points have medium confidence and unknown entrance accuracy.
See paseos-sources-v1.json, paseos-branch-crosswalk-*.json, paseos-point-proof-*.json and source-to-notice.json for the source ledger.
