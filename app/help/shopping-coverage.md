# Shopping source records and coverage

November 2, 2026. The planner presents independent source records, not a verified nationwide directory of unique active shops. Overture, AllThePlaces and OpenStreetMap can describe the same physical branch. Both shopping packages are selected by default; change them independently in **Search & filters → Shopping → Shopping sources**. The three main Sauna, Food and Shopping layers remain independent.

The main shopping package is the reviewed licensed-national-r1 increment: 8,652 source records across the approved brand groups, with 4,477 raw-hours references. It adds 1,958 dated licensed records, retains 6,676 prior records unchanged, updates 18 supported Nitori EXPRESS formats and excludes two prior Kobe records outside the current retail scope. All 85 reviewed groups and 171 member identities keep their primaries. Saved keys, flags, overrides and repeated itinerary visits are preserved, including snapshots of excluded records. These are partial, overlapping source records, not newly verified physical shops. The independent OSM package has no aliases with main shopping data.

A separate licensed catalogue contains 491 entries that are not added as map pins; withheld points are not guessed. Five held UNIQLO identities remain excluded and four dated lifecycle warnings remain explicit. Numeric references and lifecycle notices do not establish current operation, a complete calendar or entrance accuracy.

Costco has 75 raw records: 38 retained legacy identities and 37 direct warehouse records. Eleven supported groups give 64 presentation rows, which are not 64 physical warehouses; 27 legacy associations remain unresolved. Direct AllThePlaces records are labelled separately from Overture records, including Overture records that use ATP hours references. Shibuya's three grouped Hands records retain the announced November 15, 2026 final business day and 19:00 closing, with current operation still unknown. Lopia COTOE remains excluded pending resolution; an announced reopening does not automatically restore it.

The separate OSM-r2 package has 3,720 source features: 2,403 medium-confidence nodes and 1,317 low-confidence way/relation centres, not verified entrances. It has 535 raw-hours references, 568 partial community address strings and 501 prefecture values. An address string may contain only a city, building or floor. Missing addresses stay unknown. Three conflicting live/disused-tag cases remain unknown operation, with explicit source warnings.

Raw package counts total 12,372 source identities; that is not a unique-shop or active-branch count. Known source-closed records (one primary record and 12 OSM records) are excluded from discovery by default. **Include source-recorded closed places** shows them for historical/source review. Unknown operation remains included and is not evidence that a shop is open. Scheduled visits/pins remain visible regardless of discovery filters.

Hours are unverified reference text. There is no inferred weekday/holiday calendar, no guarantee of current opening and no automatic itinerary scheduling. Saved visits retain their source snapshot until you explicitly review/apply changes. Verify the exact branch, address, entrance, current hours and transport before travel.

The Overture-based component retains its CDLA/Apache/CC0 and source-specific fact notices. The independent OSM component remains under ODbL, with © OpenStreetMap contributors credit, exact extract/algorithm/modification metadata and the complete selected derivative. Source access is available on Discover, Saved and Itinerary records and in source-complete exports. Source records and selection exports remain labelled by package; private trip notes remain a separate component. No OSM fields are joined into primary records.

A complete offline ZIP requires the exact originating OSM source version. Current/previous source caches are bounded; if an older version is absent, the app refuses to substitute current data. JSON backups retain original snapshots, complete stored notices and immutable source offers. JSON backup/import remains limited to 20MiB and source-complete ZIPs to 30MiB; oversized exports must use smaller selections rather than drop records or notices.

Full national inventory, hours/status and duplicate reconciliation remains ongoing. The prior numeric-fill, GU and combined-roster packages remain published as historical source evidence. This frozen release includes only reviewed additions and null-only numerical hours fills; broader nationwide reconciliation continues. Source-complete ZIP resources cover OSM; no complete AllThePlaces raw-input archive is offered. The supplied main-package notices retain the selected licensed Costco input objects.
